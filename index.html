Safekeep

Save your tabs in one click. Never lose them.

Safekeep saves a window's tabs as a list and closes them, so you can open them again whenever you want. It's built so your saved tabs don't get lost, even when something goes wrong.

  • Nothing gets lost. Deleted tabs wait in a 30-day trash, and a backup of everything is saved to your Downloads folder every day: a file to restore from, and a page of links that opens anywhere.
  • Easy to switch to. Import from OneTab, Session Buddy, Tabs Outliner or a bookmarks file.
  • Runs on your computer. No account, no tracking, no servers.
  • The Outliner, a one-time purchase. Your open windows and saved lists in one tree, with folders, notes and drag and drop.
