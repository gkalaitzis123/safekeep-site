Safekeep

Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

In short: Safekeep keeps everything on your computer. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no Safekeep server. The only time it contacts anyone is when you activate or remove a Safekeep Pro license key.

What Safekeep stores

Safekeep saves the following in Chrome's storage on your computer. None of it is synced to your other devices, and all of it is deleted when you remove Safekeep.

  • The tabs you save: each tab's address and title, its tab group's name and color, and whether it was pinned.
  • Your lists' names, and with Safekeep Pro, your folders and notes.
  • The trash: tabs and lists you delete or open, kept for 30 days so you can put them back, then deleted for good.
  • Your settings, when the last backup was made and how many tabs it held, and which parts of the Outliner you've expanded.
  • If you activate Safekeep Pro: your license key, its activation ID and the customer name on your purchase.

If you turn on automatic backups, Safekeep also saves backup files of your lists to the “Safekeep backups” folder in your Downloads folder. They stay on your computer like any other download, and removing Safekeep doesn't delete them.

What Safekeep can see

  • Your tabs' addresses and titles. Safekeep reads them when you save tabs, to count them in its toolbar menu, and to show your open windows in the Outliner. It keeps only the tabs you save, on your computer, and never sends them anywhere.
  • Your tab groups, so saved tabs come back in their groups, with the same names and colors.
  • Your Downloads folder, only if you turn on backups. Chrome asks for your permission first, and Safekeep only saves its own backup files there.
  • Site icons from Chrome's own cache, to show next to saved tabs. No request is made to the sites.
  • Private (incognito) windows are never saved, even if you allow Safekeep in them.

What Safekeep sends

Nothing, with one exception. When you activate or remove a Safekeep Pro license key, Safekeep sends the key and its activation ID to Lemon Squeezy, which sells Safekeep Pro and checks license keys. As with any website, Lemon Squeezy also sees your IP address. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy covers what it does with that.

You buy Safekeep Pro on Lemon Squeezy's checkout page, so Safekeep never sees your payment details.

What Safekeep never does

  • No analytics, tracking or ads.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No code loaded from the internet. Everything Safekeep runs comes with the extension.

Safekeep's use of the data it can access follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Remove Safekeep from Chrome to delete everything it stored, except backup files in your Downloads folder, which are yours to keep or delete.
  • Empty the trash in Safekeep to delete things for good before their 30 days are up.
  • Turn automatic backups on or off, and export your saved tabs as a file or web page, from Safekeep's settings.
  • Remove your license key from a browser in Safekeep's settings, which also frees that activation for use elsewhere.
